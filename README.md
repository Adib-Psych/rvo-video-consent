# RVO MHPSS Surge Mechanism video: online consent form

A two-step web form for participants in the RVO MHPSS Surge Mechanism video filmed in Nepal in November 2026 (production: Makmende Media).

1. **About the video**: what the video is, who will see it, what is and is not filmed.
2. **Consent form**: the Makmende Media consent form / quit claim, completed and signed on the phone.

On submission the page builds a PDF of the completed form on the participant's device, saves it there, and sends one copy by email to the video coordinators for safekeeping. The page stores no data and this repository holds no participant data.

Live page: https://adib-psych.github.io/rvo-video-consent/
